# Security Policy

## Scope

WhatsApp Traceability Research Lab is a research prototype, not a production tracing or forensic product. The current implementation handles synthetic trace graphs, local evidence-manifest integrity, a configurable multi-approver policy demonstration, and local Merkle inclusion proofs. No production cryptographic tracing protocol is implemented. The policy and transparency components are research prototypes, not legal authorization or a production transparency service.

## Report a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue containing exploit details, private keys, real message contents, phone numbers, device images, personal data, or law-enforcement case material. Use a private vulnerability-reporting channel if one is configured for the repository; otherwise contact the maintainers through a verified project contact before disclosure. The public repo should not list an invented email address.

## Handling rules

- Do not test against WhatsApp or any real person's account without written authorization.
- Do not submit personal or case-derived data to public issue trackers or CI.
- Keep reproductions synthetic and scrubbed.
- Assume the prototype is unsuitable for legal evidence handling until an external security review and operational controls are completed.
- Follow coordinated disclosure and applicable law.
